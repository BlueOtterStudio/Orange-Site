Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Orange: Chinese Study Tools mobile application (the “App”), created by Blue Otter Studio (the “Service Provider”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in an open and transparent manner.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to provide and improve the App’s features.

a) Information You Provide Directly

  • Email Address. When you create an account (e.g., by signing in with Apple, Google, or an email-based login), we may receive your email address from the authentication provider. We use your email solely to identify your account, sync your subscriptions across devices, and provide customer support.
  • Purchase Information.All payments are processed by the official app store (Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not collect or store your credit card details, billing address, or full payment information. We receive a non-identifiable transaction identifier and a record of the purchase from the app store to unlock the paid content within the App.

b) Information Collected Automatically (Camera & Photos)

  • Camera & Pictures. The App may request access to your device’s photos to let you put photos for study purposes (e.g. flashcard creation). When you grant permission, the App captures and stores these photos exclusively on your device. The images are processed entirely on-device; they are never uploaded to our servers, shared with third parties, or transmitted over the internet. You can revoke camera access at any time through your device’s settings.

c) Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising tools (such as Google Analytics).
  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or logs of your activity.
  • We do not send marketing or promotional emails. If you contact us by email, we will only use your email address to respond to your inquiry.
  • We do not show ads or use remarketing services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To process in-app purchases and unlock paid features.
  • To communicate with you regarding support requests you initiate.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers. We use the official app store’s payment systems (Apple/Google) to process transactions. These providers may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive your full payment details.
  • Legal Requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests (e.g., a court order).
  • Business Transfers. In the event of a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction, provided the successor agrees to honor this Privacy Policy.

We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately. We will take steps to delete such information from our records.

No part of the App is designed to attract anyone under 13, and we fully comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data.

a) For European Economic Area (EEA) and UK Residents – GDPR

If you are located in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to:

  • Access – Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification – Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (“Right to be forgotten”) – Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction of Processing – Ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Data Portability – Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection – Object to processing based on legitimate interests (we do not rely on legitimate interests for any processing; we process data only with your consent or to fulfill our contract).
  • Withdraw Consent – Where processing is based on consent (e.g., account creation), you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Lawful Basis for Processing:

  • Performance of a Contract: We process your email and purchase information to provide the services you requested (account, access to paid features).
  • Consent: Camera access is based on your explicit consent, which you grant through the device permissions and can revoke at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at orange-app@outlook.com. We will respond within one calendar month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), California residents have the following rights:

  • Right to Delete: You can ask us to delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You can request correction of inaccurate personal information.

  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: We do not collect or process sensitive personal information (as defined by CPRA).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

Categories of Personal Information Collected in the Last 12 Months:

  • Identifiers (e.g., email address)
  • Commercial Information (records of products/services purchased)
  • Audio, electronic, visual, or similar information (photos you take within the App; stored solely on your device)

We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months. We only use and disclose information for the business purposes described in this policy.

To exercise any California privacy rights, please email us at orange-app@outlook.com with the subject “CCPA Request.” We will verify your identity before processing the request. You may also designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf.
