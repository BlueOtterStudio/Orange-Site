Contact

For general inquiries, partnership or press: orange-app@outlook.com

Support requests

For product issues or bugs, email orange-app@outlook.com with a clear description, your device model, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. This helps us triage and respond faster.

Account deletion & customer support

For account deletion requests and any customer support inquiries (billing, account access, data removal, or general assistance), please contact us at orange-app@outlook.com. Include the email address associated with your account and any relevant details so we can process your request promptly and in accordance with applicable privacy regulations.

Security issues

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please email orange-app@outlook.com privately and include reproduction details. Do not post security issues publicly.

Response time

We aim to respond to support, account deletion, and security inquiries within 3 business days. Response times may vary depending on volume.
